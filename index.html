What we do

Show the real work. Leave with something you can use Monday.

We're the people who make software products on Vancouver Island. We get together, in small groups, to show each other how the work actually gets done, what's changing, and what we're learning as we go.

What you getA small, trusted room of Island product makers where you'll see how peers really build, test and ship, and go home with something you can use Monday.

  • Show the real work, and leave it behind.

    Share what you actually built and how you actually decided, messy parts included. Every gathering leaves something the group keeps: a workflow, a template, a lesson.

    Not here keynotes, vendor pitches, socials with nothing to show for them.

  • Everyone in the room when it ships.

    Product is a team sport. Designers, engineers, testers, support and PMs get the same seat and the same mic.

    Not here PM-only circles, seniority hierarchies.

  • Say the true thing.

    What failed matters as much as what worked, especially now, when everyone is pretending they've figured out AI.

    Not here hype, humblebragging, sanitized case studies.

  • Small on purpose.

    We'd rather have twenty people who know each other's work than two hundred who don't. Trust is the product.

    Not here growth for its own sake.

  • Island first, open door.

    Our members are here. Guests can be from anywhere, as long as they come to share how they work rather than sell what they make.

    Not here sponsor-driven programming.
